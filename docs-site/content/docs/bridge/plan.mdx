The Bridge is one LCARS view per Herdr space, and a space is one worktree. It shows the change that is being
built in that worktree, with the data from `striker serve`. Three pseudo-tabs at the left of the tab bar switch
its mode: **OVERVIEW**, **PLAN** and **SPEC**. The shown one is orange, the others peach. A click on one of
the space's real tabs leaves the Bridge and shows that terminal again.

In a linked worktree (a change's `x-branch`), the Bridge opens on PLAN. On the main checkout it opens on
[OVERVIEW](#overview-the-repo-one-level-up).

![PLAN of the demo change add-greeting: tasks 1 and 2 done, task 3 open with its steps and the step it is on](/shots/plan.png)

## The elbow and its rows

PLAN draws the change's [`tasks.md`](/docs/concepts/openspec-workflow) as an orange LCARS elbow. The arm at the top
carries the change's title; under it there is one row per task, separated by thin darker lines.

A row shows:

- an **oval** for the task's state: blue working, red blinking when it needs you, gold done, gray interrupted, and
  orange with a black outline for a task nobody has started;
- the task's **id and title** (`TASK 3 · README USAGE SECTION`);
- a **chevron**, `▸` closed, `▾` open.

**Click on a row:** it opens like an accordion and closes the others. An open row turns black with orange text and
lists the task's steps from `plan.md` as plain text, `☑` for a done step and `☐` for an open one. The first open step
is marked `◂ NOW`, whether or not an agent is working on it.

**Click on the title in the arm:** shows the change's coordinator in the bottom field. The title is only clickable
while a coordinator runs for the change.

## The three columns

Right of the elbow stand three square column headings: **WORKING** (blue), **NEEDS YOU** (red) and **DONE** (gold).
Each task has at most one card, and that card sits on the task's own row, in the column of its state:

| Task state | Column | Card | Click |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| working | WORKING | blue strip, the agent's tab and `STEP n/m`, a step bar | Shows the agent's terminal in the bottom field |
| needs you | NEEDS YOU | red, blinking strip, the agent's tab and `NEEDS YOU` | Shows the agent's terminal, so you can answer there |
| interrupted | WORKING | gray strip, `INTERRUPTED · NOBODY ON IT` | – |
| done | DONE | gold strip, `DONE` | – |
| not started | – | no card | – |

A task counts as **interrupted** when it is the first open task after ticked ones and nothing runs for the whole
change: no task agent, no coordinator, and no lead working on it. Work stopped there, and nobody picked it up.
That is not an alarm (it is gray, not red), but it tells you where the coordinator would go on.

Several cards in WORKING at once mean that the coordinator dispatched task agents in parallel: tasks whose
`after` dependencies are all done can run side by side. In the demo change `add-greeting`, tasks 2 and 3 both come
after task 1, so once task 1 is done they run at the same time (see [OpenSpec workflow](/docs/concepts/openspec-workflow)).

The bottom field then shows the agent you clicked, under a rule that names it: `TASK 2 · IMPLEMENTER · LIVE`
(blue, or red for a task that needs you). The round **◂ REPO** button at the right of the rule brings the
[REPO tree](/docs/bridge/repo) back.

## A read-only PLAN

A change that has no worktree yet can still be shown in PLAN, read from the main checkout: OVERVIEW's **PLAN ▸**
button does that. It shows the same rows and cards, but nothing on it opens a terminal, because there is no
coordinator or agent to show.

## Messages instead of a plan

When there is nothing to draw, the elbow's arm says why:

| Message | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `SCANNING …` | The first answer from `striker serve` has not come yet |
| `NO PLAN FOR BRANCH <branch>` | The worktree's branch is not the `x-branch` of any change |
| `NO TASKS IN PLAN` | The change's `tasks.md` has no task lines |
| `NO REPO FOR THIS SPACE` | The space's folder is not a repo Striker knows |
| `REPO UNREADABLE: …` / `STRIKER SERVE NOT RUNNING` | `striker serve` could not answer; the last good data stays |

## OVERVIEW: the repo one level up

OVERVIEW is PLAN for the whole repo instead of one change. The elbow and the three columns are the same; the rows
are the repo's parts:

![OVERVIEW of the demo repo alpha: the change add-greeting open, its coordinator working](/shots/overview.png)

- **The arm** is taller and carries the repo's name, a briefing line (phase, next step, progress, the lead and how
  old its report is, else the last commit) and the last commits.
- **VISION · \<title\>** (black oval): opens to the vision's first paragraph and a round `<FILE> IN SPEC ▸` button
  that shows the file in [SPEC](/docs/bridge/spec).
- **One row per active change:** its oval, its name, `done/total`, `AFTER <change>` while it waits for another
  change, `READY TO ARCHIVE` when every task is ticked and nobody is on it. Open, it lists the `proposal.md` and
  `design.md` headings and the task lines with `☑`/`☐` and `◂ NOW`. Its round **PLAN ▸** button selects the
  change's worktree space in PLAN, or shows PLAN read-only here when the change has no worktree.
- **REGISTER:** the open items of the lead (its `needs_you` and its register), red while there are any.
- **DONE:** the archived changes, newest first.
- **CAPABILITIES:** the repo's living specs, as one line under the rows.

A change row's card is its coordinator: WORKING with its task and a tick bar, NEEDS YOU with the coordinator's
question, gray INTERRUPTED with the last ticked task, or gold READY TO ARCHIVE. A click on a coordinator's card
shows its terminal in the bottom field.

The colour of a change's oval, card and tree lane follows one rule:

| Colour | When |
| --- | --- |
| Red, blinking | Its coordinator asks you |
| Blue | Someone is on it now: its coordinator, one of its task agents, or the lead |
| Gold | Every task ticked, not archived yet |
| Gray | Interrupted (ticked and open tasks, nobody on it) or blocked by another change |
| Outline only | Planned: nothing ticked, nobody on it |

Blue never means "in progress" on its own: a change nobody works on right now is gold, gray or an outline.
