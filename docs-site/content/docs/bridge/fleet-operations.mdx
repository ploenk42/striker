Fleet Operations is Striker's board inside the LCARS frame. It has one column for each repo of the fleet, and
it shows what you would otherwise ask Striker for: which lead is working, what each lead and coordinator is on,
and what waits on you.

![Fleet Operations with the demo fleet: alpha and beta, each with its lead waiting on you, alpha with its coordinator. A third column (Striker's own repo) is covered.](/shots/fleet-operations.png)

## Opening it

- The **STRIKER** button in the sidebar switches between the board and the terminals. Its colour already tells you
  something: red and pulsing while a repo needs you (`02 NEED YOU`), gold otherwise (`02 REPOS`), orange while the
  board is shown (it then reads **TERMINALS**).
- The **STRIKER** count in the status row at the bottom (`01 STRIKER`) opens the board as well. It counts the repos
  that need you.

The board is drawn in the work view, the same place that otherwise shows the space's terminals. Nothing in the
fleet stops or restarts when you switch.

## Where the data comes from

The board reads `http://127.0.0.1:7522/status`, the same answer Striker reads, from
[`striker serve`](/docs/setup/install). The sidebar asks every 20 seconds and keeps the answer in shared state, so the
board, the STRIKER button and the status row always show the same thing. Striker's `/crew` answer adds the crew names
(see below). The live state of a lead or coordinator comes straight from Herdr and wins over the polled one,
because it is instant.

## The header

| Part | Means | Click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STRIKER · FLEET OPERATIONS` | The board's title on the orange frame | – |
| `SCAN <stardate>` | When `striker serve` last scanned the fleet. `· STALE` when that scan is more than a minute old or the last ask failed | – |
| **TALK TO STRIKER** | Striker's prompt | Gives the keyboard to Striker's prompt under the board (or, from elsewhere, goes to Striker's own pane) |
| **TERMINALS** | Back to the terminals | Leaves the board |

A stardate is the local clock as one number, `YYYYMMDD.hhmmss`: `20261124.143012` is 24 November 2026,
14:30:12. When `striker serve` does not answer, a red line under the header says why (`SCAN FAILED: STRIKER SERVE NOT
RUNNING`), and the board keeps showing the last good scan.

## A column

Each column belongs to one repo from [`fleet.json`](/docs/setup/fleet), plus any local lead that Striker found by its
`striker.lead` label. From top to bottom:

### Head

A block in the colour of the repo's state, with the repo's name in large letters and a small line above it:

- **who asks you**, when someone does: `LCDR PATA · NEEDS YOU` for the lead, or
  `LT WERF · ADD-GREETING · NEEDS YOU` when a coordinator asks (`· +1` when more than one does);
- otherwise the lead and its state: `LCDR PATA · WORKING`; `NO LEAD` when the repo has none. A repo without a
  lead whose status file still holds `needs_you` entries reads `LEAD · NEEDS YOU` instead.

The colour follows the [LCARS rule](#colours): red and blinking when the lead or one of its coordinators needs
you, gold when the lead's phase is `done` (the word then reads `COMPLETE`), blue while it works, gray when it is
idle or unknown.

**Click:** the head goes to whoever asks you (the lead, else the first asking coordinator), otherwise to the
lead. A head with neither (no lead, no asking coordinator) does nothing.

### Body

The body scrolls when a repo has more to say than the column is tall. It holds, in this order:

1. **What waits on you:** one line for each `needs_you` entry in the lead's status file, with a red tag for its
   kind (`ANTRAG`, `ABNAHME`, `FRAGE`, `RECHT`; any other kind in capitals, `FRAGE` when none is given) and the
   text. A click goes to the lead, when there is one.
2. **PHASE:** a row of five cells, DESIGN, PLAN, BUILD, REVIEW, PUSH. The current phase is orange, the ones before
   it tan, the rest dark. It is hidden while the lead is idle or done.
3. **TASK:** what the lead says it is on (`NO TASK` when it says nothing).
4. **PROGRESS:** a segmented bar with `done/total`, when the status file gives a progress.
5. **STATUS:** the lead's last note.

`NO STATUS FILE YET` means the lead runs but has not written its [status file](/docs/reference/status-file) yet;
`STATUS: …` in red means the file could not be read.

### Lead line

Under the body: a strip in the lead's state colour, `LCDR PATA · WORKING`, and under it the lead's terminal
address, its last message and how long ago its status file was updated (`UPDATED 3 MIN AGO`). **Click:** goes to
the lead's terminal. A repo without a lead shows a gray block `NO LEAD · ASK STRIKER` instead: you start one through
Striker ([First lead](/docs/setup/first-lead)).

### Coordinator lines

One line for each coordinator of the repo, one per change: `LT WERF · ADD-GREETING · WORKING`, and under it
its terminal address, its worktree folder, its task and when it last updated its status file. What a coordinator
asks you is listed under its line, with the same red kind tags. **Click:** on the line or on one of its questions,
goes to the coordinator's terminal.

A coordinator whose status file cannot be read stays listed, with the error, but never in red: red means that
someone needs you, not that a file is broken.

### Crew names

The names come from Striker's `/crew` answer: every agent gets a rank and a name (`LCDR PATA`, `LT WERF`), so
you can tell them apart at a glance. When every name of both lists is taken, an agent is called `CREWMAN <n>`
(as the coordinator `LT CREWMAN 1` in the shot). The same names appear in the sidebar's CREW list. Until Striker has named an
agent, its line falls back to `LEAD` or `COORD <change>`.

## Colours

The board adds no colours of its own. Status colours mean one thing each:

| Colour | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Red, blinking | Waits on you: a `needs_you` entry, or a lead or coordinator whose terminal is blocked |
| Blue | Working |
| Gold | Finished |
| Gray | Nothing to report: idle, no lead, zero |
| Orange, tan, peach | Frame and labels only, never a status |

The full table is in `docs/lcars-colors.md` in the repository.
